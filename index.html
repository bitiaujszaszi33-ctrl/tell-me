BEFORE YOU BEGIN

How to play

❧
01

Choose a question

Take turns answering the question shown on the card. There are 100 questions in the deck.

02

Be honest

There are no right or wrong answers. Share only what you feel comfortable sharing.

03

Move through the deck

Press Next question or the → arrow to move forward. Press the ← arrow to return to the previous question.

04

Shuffle the deck

Use Shuffle whenever you want the questions to appear in a new order.

05

No pressure

You can always skip a question. The goal is to create meaningful conversations, not uncomfortable ones.
